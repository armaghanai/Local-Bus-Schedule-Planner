About

This planner is for students and travellers who need a quick way to check bus times and fares. It runs entirely in your browser. The timetable repeats daily and the data is sample data based on public timetables.

Disclaimer: This is an independent student project for educational purposes. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Faisal Movers in any way. "Faisal Movers" is used only to name the sample timetable data.

All times and fares are sample data and may not be accurate. Check the official Faisal Movers website or terminal for live schedules and bookings.
